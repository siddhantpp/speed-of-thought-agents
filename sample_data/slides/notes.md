survey
- google form in the Tamsin Hall group chat + asked ppl in the Larkmoor Commons line at lunch, Oct 26-30
- 40 first-years answered (only first-years count, dropped 3 sophomores)
- ~~35 of 40 still on the 19 plan~~ RECOUNT tues: 31 of 40 (I double counted the thurs sheet) = 78%

  14 plan: avg 10.4 used, 3.6 unused
  10 plan: avg 8.7 used, 1.3 unused
- so ppl on the default eat about the same as the ones who switched (11.2 vs 10.4) but pay for 19

why still on 19? (the 31, pick your main reason)

- "I actually want 19" 2 (6%)
-> endorsement biggest, effort 2nd. matches the class answer!
