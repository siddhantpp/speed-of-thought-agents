Working title: "Nineteen Meals by Default"

## Key messages (all of these must land)
1. The default: first-years are put on the largest meal plan, 19 meals a week, unless they switch.
2. It sticks: 31 of the 40 first-years I surveyed are still on the default plan.
3. It doesn't fit: people on the default use 11.2 meals a week on average, so 7.8 of their 19 go unused, and the default costs $260 more per semester than the 14-meal plan.
4. Why it sticks: endorsement first (14 of the 31 figured 19 was the recommended amount), then effort (9 meant to switch and never did).
5. Recommendation: make the 14-meal plan the default, keep the two-week switch window, and send a one-click reminder in week 1.
6. Caveat: a small, self-reported, non-random survey. Treat it as a pilot, not proof.
