# Research question

Should I take lecture notes by hand or on a laptop? What does the research actually show?

## What Riley needs

- A short evidence report of about 800 to 1,200 words that a student can act on.
- At least 5 credible sources: peer-reviewed journal articles or reviews, found and fetched through the research tools.
- Every factual claim cited with a numbered source like [1]; quotes only if copied word for word from that source's abstract.
- An honest account of how strong the evidence is, including whether well-known findings held up when others repeated them.
